Privacy Policy for Tessera

Last updated: 13 September 2026

Tessera (“the Game”, package com.zengothic.tessera) is a single-player tile puzzle game published by Zengothic (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the Game and the services inside it handle, and what choices you have.

The short version: we run no servers, there are no accounts, and we never receive any personal information about you. Your progress stays on your phone and is not even backed up off it. The Game does show advertisements, and the Google advertising service that delivers them collects data as described below.

1. Information we collect ourselves

None. The Game has no sign-in, no user profiles, no chat, no leaderboards, no cloud save and no analytics of our own. We operate no servers, so no information about you is ever transmitted to us.

2. Information stored on your device

The Game saves your progress locally, under a single key (tessera_save_v1), so you can continue where you left off:

  • Which level you last played
  • Which levels you have completed, your best move count and play count on each
  • How many hints you used per level
  • Which tutorial lines you have already seen
  • Whether sound is on or off, and whether reduced motion is on or off

That is the complete list. It contains no identifier of any kind — no name, no device id, no random id that could follow you.

Unlike many apps, this data is not copied off your device by Android Backup. The Game sets allowBackup="false" and excludes itself from device-to-device transfer, so the save stays on the phone that made it. Uninstalling the Game deletes it.

3. Advertising

The Game is free and is funded by advertising. We use Google AdMob to show it. We do not run an ad server and we receive no personal information from it, but Google does process data in order to serve and measure ads, including an advertising identifier and general information about your device.

What the Game actually shows:

FormatWhen
Full-screen ad between levelsAfter a number of completed levels, and never less than three minutes apart
Optional rewarded adOnly if you choose it, from a “Stuck?” offer on a board you have genuinely stalled on

There is no banner ad and no ad when the app opens. Tessera runs straight from one board to the next with no menu screen, so there is nowhere a banner could sit without covering the puzzle you are playing.

Google’s handling of advertising data is described in its own policies:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • AdMob and data collection

4. Your choices about ads

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the Game shows Google’s official consent form before requesting personalised ads, and you can reopen it at any time from the ? menu to change your answer.
  • On Android, your device has an advertising-ID control in its own settings — Settings → Privacy → Ads — where you can reset the identifier or turn off ad personalisation for every app at once, Tessera included.
  • On iPhone, Tessera asks once, through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt, whether ads may use your device’s advertising identifier. If you say no, ads are not personalised and the Game works exactly the same. You can change this any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

The VIP purchase (iPhone)

On iPhone, Tessera offers one optional one-time purchase, VIP: no ads between levels and three free level skips a day. Payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store; we never see your card, Apple ID or any payment details. The Game only learns whether VIP has been bought, so it can turn the ads off, and keeps that on your device. Restore purchases (in the ? menu) brings VIP back on a new or reset device.

5. Permissions

Tessera asks for no permission to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or files. It does need network access, because ads have to be fetched. Every permission below arrives with Google’s Mobile Ads SDK rather than with the game’s own code, but they ship in the app, so here they are in full:

PermissionWhat it is for
INTERNETFetching ads. No gameplay feature uses the network
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATEChecking whether there is a connection before asking for an ad
com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_IDThe advertising identifier
ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_IDAndroid’s Privacy Sandbox advertising-ID API
ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTIONPrivacy Sandbox ad attribution (measuring whether an ad led to an install)
ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICSPrivacy Sandbox Topics — interest signals used for ad relevance
FOREGROUND_SERVICE, WAKE_LOCKDeclared by Google Play Services components the Ads SDK depends on

The build also contains com.zengothic.tessera.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION, which is easy to misread. It is defined by the app, for the app — note it carries Tessera’s own package name rather than android.permission.*. Its protection level is signature, so only code signed with the same key can hold it. Android’s own AndroidX libraries add it automatically; it requests no capability, accesses no data, and is never shown to you as a prompt. It makes the app more locked down, not less.

6. Children

Tessera has no chat and no user-generated content, its one purchase is optional, and the puzzle itself is suitable for any age. It does carry third-party advertising, so it is not directed at children and is not enrolled in Google Play’s Families programme.

7. Deleting your data

Everything Tessera itself stores is on your device. You can erase all of it at any time: open the ? button in the game and choose Reset progress, or uninstall the app, which removes the stored data with it.

We hold nothing about you on any server, so there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf. Data held by Google in connection with advertising is governed by Google’s policy linked above, and by your device’s advertising-ID controls.
